OUR STORY

A family-managedhotel in the old citycentre

Makadam Hotel takes its name from the cobblestone roads of Prizren — surfaces laid stone by stone, time-worn yet timeless.

Set at Rruga Berati 16, just one minute on foot from Shadërvan Square, our intimate family-managed hotel places guests in the heart of Prizren Old Town. Every stay includes a private bathroom, air conditioning, Smart TV and Wi-Fi, with selected rooms opening onto a balcony or terrace. Guests can also enjoy our private pine-tree yard and grill area, while massage and physiotherapy sessions are available by advance reservation.

Stay with us, in the historic heart of Prizren.
